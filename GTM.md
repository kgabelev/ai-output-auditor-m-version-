## Competitive Positioning

### vs. Vendor Tools (Basis, Fieldguide, Numeric)
**They:** Build confidence scores into their tools
**You:** Independent evaluation of ANY tool
**Win:** Firm doesn't have to pick one vendor; you validate all

### vs. Manual Review
**They:** Accountants manually review AI output
**You:** Automate 50-70% of review; accountants only handle exceptions
**Win:** 10x faster close, documented evidence for audit

### vs. Generic AI Evals
**They:** "LLM judges output"
**You:** Deterministic checks + regulatory-mapped rubrics
**Win:** Accounting is binary; your approach proves it

## Sales Script (2-Minute Pitch)

> "Accounting firms are racing to adopt AI but have a real problem: they can't trust the output. Our research shows the models they use fail 15% of the time, but the failures look confident. That's an audit liability.
> 
> AOA solves this. It's an independent evaluation tool that doesn't care which AI your firm uses. You load invoices or reconciliations, it gives you verdicts backed by evidence and regulatory citations. The stuff that passes posts immediately. The stuff that fails gets clear next steps.
> 
> We've mapped the evaluation to Circular 230, AICPA standards, and PCAOB requirements, so your work is defensible. And it gets better over time — we track accuracy by category so you know which evaluations are rock-solid and which need tuning.
> 
> Want to see it work?"
